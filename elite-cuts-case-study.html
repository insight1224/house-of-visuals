Project Overview

Elite Cuts Barbershop is a concept project created to show how a local grooming business can use a professional website, strong branding, and clear calls-to-action to bring in more bookings. The design focuses on confidence, style, trust, and convenience.

Industry

Barbershop / Grooming

Services

Website Design, Branding Direction, Booking Strategy, Mobile Design

Goal

Help the business look professional online and make it easy for customers to book appointments.

The Challenge

Many local barbershops rely heavily on word of mouth and social media, but their websites are either outdated, hard to use, or missing completely. This concept solves that problem by creating a polished online home where customers can see services, view the brand personality, and book quickly.
