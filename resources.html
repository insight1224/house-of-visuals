Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between DIY Template and Done For You Setup?

DIY gives you the template files to customize yourself. Done For You means House of Visuals customizes everything for you.

Can I preview the template before buying?

Yes. Use the Live Preview area to view sample structures and visual direction before selecting an option.

Can House of Visuals customize the template for me?

Absolutely. Choose Done For You Setup and we’ll tailor it to your brand and goals.

Do DIY templates include setup help?

DIY templates include guidance notes. If you need more support, we can help through consultation.

Can I use my own logo, colors, and photos?

Yes. All templates are built to be customized with your own brand assets.

How does checkout work?

DIY template checkout is currently a placeholder route while we finalize instant-access delivery.

What if I need something fully custom?

We offer fully custom websites, systems, and creative services. Use the Done For You route to start.
