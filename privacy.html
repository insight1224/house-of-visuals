Privacy Policy

This page explains how House of Visuals Co. handles information sent through the forms on this website.

House of Visuals Co. is a web design studio serving Raleigh, Durham, and the Triangle. The public contact, testimonial, and Print by HOV forms send what you submit to hello@houseofvisualsco.com through FormSubmit.

Those details are used only to read your message and reply. That includes project inquiries, testimonial submissions, and print-order requests. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with other businesses for their marketing.

This website does not run analytics and does not use advertising cookies. FormSubmit processes the submission so it can be delivered by email. Please do not send passwords or payment card numbers through these forms.

To ask a privacy question or request a copy or deletion of a message you sent, email hello@houseofvisualsco.com.

This policy was last updated in November 2026.
