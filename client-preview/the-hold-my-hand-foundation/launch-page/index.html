Our Mission

Guiding young people toward hope, resilience, and better decisions.

The Hold My Hand Foundation was created to empower youth through real-life experiences, mentorship, life skills, and compassionate community support.

We want every young person to know that their current circumstances do not have to determine the rest of their story.

What We Do

Focused support for youth and families.

Our work centers on helping young people develop confidence, practical skills, and a stronger sense of direction.

01

Mentorship

Caring guidance and encouragement that helps youth recognize their potential and make positive choices.

02

Life Skills

Practical experiences that build confidence, responsibility, communication, leadership, and decision-making skills.

03

Community Support

Strong partnerships, safe spaces, and helpful resources for youth, families, and the surrounding community.

Why It Matters

A personal story transformed into a mission to help others.

The foundation grew from a life-changing experience that inspired its founder to help young people understand the importance of their choices, recognize their value, and know they do not have to face life alone.

“Your current chapter does not have to determine the rest of your story.”
