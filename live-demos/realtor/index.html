Website + Dashboard Showcase

Homepage: Property-focused hero section with search, trust indicators, featured listings, and lead-focused CTAs.

Properties Page: Listing-focused layout designed for easy browsing and stronger lead capture.

Property Details Page: High-converting property detail view with key features and clear next steps.

About Page: Trust-building brand story with values, client-first messaging, local expertise, and consultation CTAs.

Seller Valuation Page: Home value pathway that captures seller interest and turns homeowners into qualified leads.

Consultation Flow: Multi-step booking experience for property interest, preferred time, contact details, and review.

Lead Form: Consultation request page that captures buyer or seller goals, budget, timeline, and contact details.

Confirmation Page: Polished thank-you screen with next steps, contact details, and clear paths back to the site.

Pipeline Dashboard: Operations view with lead stages, client tasks, appointments, analytics, and performance details.

Schedule Dashboard: Daily calendar view for buyer consultations, showings, listing presentations, and team meetings.

Client Notes: Follow-up hub for client communication, property preferences, feedback, reminders, and saved notes.

Commissions Dashboard: Earnings view for payouts, pending commissions, outstanding balances, and transaction tracking.
